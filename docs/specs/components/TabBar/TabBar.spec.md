# TabBar component contract

## Contract at a glance

| Area                    | Contract                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Public contract         | `TabBar` takes `moreValue` (default `'more'`); `TabBarMoreList` takes `children`, `value` and `onChange`; `createTabBar`, and `useTabBar` over it, hand a bar the app draws the same behavior as values and props; Core's catalog has `@astryx.tabBar.more`.                                                                                                                                                |

## Intent

TabBar is the lab's floating bar of top-level sections, modelled on UIKit's
tab bar. This record states how it holds more sections than fit, how disabled
tabs meet the keyboard, and the More tab's label. An app that draws its own
bar gets the same behavior from `createTabBar`, a factory that works without
React, or from `useTabBar`, its thin hook. They hand out values and props and
leave every element to the app. Consumer usage lives in `TabBar.doc.mjs`.

## Compatibility and migration

- Released default preserved: `no` while the on-screen keyboard is up (FR12); otherwise `yes` for a bar of five tabs or fewer. A bar of six or more tabs, outside the documented three to five, moves its fifth tab onward into More (FR1).
- Compatibility class: additive; `moreValue`, `TabBarMoreList`, `createTabBar` and `useTabBar` are new.
- Controlled/uncontrolled behavior: unchanged; the app owns `value`.
- Migration decision: none needed.

## Ownership boundary

**Owns**

- Which tabs the bar shows, and when More replaces the rest.
- When More reads as selected, and what choosing More again means.
- `TabBarMoreList`: More's list of the remaining tabs.
- `createTabBar` and `useTabBar`: the bar's behavior, as values and props, for a bar the app draws.

**Does not own / non-goals**

- More's navigation stack and the screens inside it — owned by the app.
- Rearranging tabs (iOS's Edit) — not provided.
- The look of a bar the app draws, and its lens's motion — owned by the app, which may animate it with `useAnimator` (`spec:AST-107` FR3).
- An action beside the tabs, such as a compose button — the app's own control (FR11).

### Allowed variation

- **AV1 — More's icon.** The icon may change with the theme; the slot and label may not.

### Representative states

| State                                   | Required invariant                                          | Allowed variation          |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Five tabs                               | No More tab (FR1)                                           | None                       |
| Six tabs, a fifth selected              | More selected; its list shows the fifth selected (FR2, FR4) | None                       |
| A disabled tab past the fourth          | Its row is disabled (FR5)                                   | Its dimmed paint           |
| A drawn bar, a finger dragging the lens | Lens state `dragging`, over the tab under the finger (FR9)  | The lens's look and motion |
| A drawn bar with a compose action       | The action is outside the tab list (FR11)                   | Its place beside the tabs  |
| A field focused, the keyboard up        | The bar is not drawn over or above the keyboard (FR12)      | None                       |

### Transformation and precedence order

- **ORD1 — selection.** A tab past the fourth resolves to More before the lens places itself (FR2).

### Performance and resources

- **PR1 — no extra listeners.** The More tab adds no listener beyond those of a tab.
- **PR2 — one implementation.** A page that renders `TabBar` carries the factory and the hook as part of the component (FR10). A page that uses only `createTabBar` or `useTabBar` carries no bar styles, capsule or lens drawing.

## Accessibility contract

- **AR1 — one name per row.** A More row with a badge MUST be named by `@astryx.tabBar.badgedItem`, as its tab is.
- **AR2 — named list.** `TabBarMoreList` MUST be named by `@astryx.tabBar.more`.
- **AR3 — a drawn bar keeps the names.** The props `useTabBar` returns, and the attributes `createTabBar` sets, MUST name the tab list and each tab as `TabBar` does, a badged tab by `@astryx.tabBar.badgedItem` (AR1) or by the factory's `labels`.

## Family and system relationships

- `spec:AST-085` owns the disabled-control rules; TabBar applies FR1, FR2 and FR5 to its tabs and their More rows.
- `spec:AST-107` DEC-1 gives products values and one method each from its gesture hooks, never the gesture machinery, and DEC-2 puts a factory under each hook. `createTabBar` and `useTabBar` follow that shape (FR8, FR9). `TabBar` is built on `createTabBar` (FR10), so `architecture:public-component-api` INV21 counts the three as one API, and INV22's non-React equivalent is the factory.
- `spec:AST-099` FR3 governs the keyboard value Core keeps internal. FR12 reads whether a keyboard is up, in a host that keeps its window and in one that resizes; a bar that rides (OQ1) also needs its height.
- `spec:AST-085` leaves the arrow keys inside a composite to its own record: "Which items arrow keys visit inside a composite widget (listbox, menu, grid, tablist, roving group). The composite's own record decides that." FR6 decides it for TabBar, as Core's TabList does.

## Decision log

### DEC-1 — An app draws its own bar on `createTabBar` or `useTabBar`

**Reference:** `component:TabBar/DEC-1`
**Decider:** pending

An app with its own look keeps the tab bar's behavior: the selection, the names
and keys, the More overflow, and the finger's lens. `createTabBar` hands these
out as values without React, and `useTabBar` is its thin hook, the layer and the
shape `spec:AST-107` DEC-1 and DEC-2 give gestures (FR8, FR9). Every element and
the lens's drawing stay the app's. `TabBar` is built entirely on the factory
(FR10), so `architecture:public-component-api` INV21 counts the three as one
API, and INV22's non-React equivalent exists.

Rejected: a `renderTab` prop and an action slot on `TabBar`, which keep the bar's capsule and lens look; a hook without a factory (INV22).

## Open questions

- **OQ1 — Hide or ride.** While the keyboard is up, FR12 keeps the bar from being
  drawn over or above it, so the bar hides. Should it instead ride fully above
  the keys? Hiding matches UIKit, where no part of the tab bar shows over the
  keyboard, and needs only whether a keyboard is up. Riding keeps the sections
  in reach while typing, and needs the keyboard's height to place the bar,
  except where the window shrinks. Both need OQ2's keyboard read; riding also
  needs the height, which `spec:AST-099` FR3 keeps internal.
- **OQ2 — Lab's keyboard read.** FR12 needs Lab to know a keyboard is up, in a
  host that keeps its window and in one that resizes for the keyboard.
  `architecture:react-component-runtime` INV11 keeps Core's keyboard measure
  unexported, and `spec:AST-099` FR2's height is 0 where the window resizes.
  Should `spec:AST-099` give Lab whether a keyboard is up, with a signal where
  the window resizes? Without that read, FR12 has no detection.
