## Intent

DateInput presents one controlled calendar date through a labeled input-field
surface. A person may type the value or use a Calendar, bottom-sheet calendar, or
browser/OS date picker selected by the public presentation contract.

## Compatibility and migration

- Released default preserved: `yes`
- Compatibility class: additive `calendar` prop with a Gregorian default; under
  a finger the sheet wheels' markup changes, and under every presentation keys
  step through the values in time order (FR17). Typed Gregorian entry fires
  `onChange` only for complete input, a nonbreaking fix that restores FR1's
  next-ISO-date contract; its rejection messages are additive; on Enter or blur,
  `parseDateInput` still reads text the field cannot otherwise read and a date
  the locale's field order lacks, and a one- or three-digit year is not a date
  (FR20). A non-Gregorian calendar replaces the native control with the Astryx
  surface for the pointer, and an explicit `native` warns in development (FR21).
  A provider locale with a `-u-ca-` keyword keeps Gregorian dates
  (`spec:AST-111` DEC-2); set `calendar` to choose one.
- Controlled/uncontrolled behavior: unchanged; DateInput remains controlled by
  `value`, with `undefined` representing no selected date
- Migration decision: `spec:AST-043/DEC-1` and `spec:AST-043/DEC-2` own the
  `presentation` vocabulary, default, deprecated `nativePicker` mapping, and
  precedence

Consumer migration instructions belong in consumer docs and release notes.

## Ownership boundary

**Owns**

- Selecting the pointer, bottom-sheet, or native field surface from the resolved
  public presentation.
- Parsing typed dates, formatting committed dates, enforcing DateInput constraints,
  and emitting one controlled ISO date or `undefined`.
- The DateInput field wrapper, calendar-toggle affordance, clear composition, and
  focus handoff between those controls and the selected picker surface.
- DateInput-specific composition of Field, Calendar, Popover, BottomSheet, and the
  browser/OS date control.
- The shared bottom-sheet picker wheel: how a wheel rests, commits, steps, turns,
  and looks wherever DateInput, TimeInput, or DateTimeInput renders it in a sheet
  (FR13–FR15, FR17, FR18, DEC-1, DEC-2), and DateInput's own month and year
  wheels (FR23).
- The loop's steps in `DateInput/wheelOptions.ts`: `restingLoopStep`, a rest's
  turn to the nearest enabled row, and `loopedStep`, a key's step across the
  seam (FR13, FR17, DEC-3).
- The disabled-reason Tooltip composition: DateInput renders the reason Tooltip
  and links it from its focusable controls (`family:input-fields`
  representative matrix, "Member with `disabledMessage`").

**Does not own / non-goals**

- Label, description, status, width, and shared clear-button presentation —
  owned by `component:Field`, `component:FieldStatus`, and `family:input-fields`.
- Calendar-grid dates, navigation, and selection semantics — owned by
  `component:Calendar`.
- Weekday header names — set by `component:Calendar` DD1; the bottom-sheet
  calendar shows them (FR19).
- Popover hosting and topmost dismissal — owned by `component:Popover` and
  `family:overlay-dismissal`.
- Bottom-sheet hosting and dismissal — owned by `component:BottomSheet` and the
  overlay-dismissal family. The bottom-sheet surface inherits BottomSheet's recorded
  shared-dismissal adoption gap.
- Cross-component picker vocabulary and deprecated-prop migration — owned by
  `spec:AST-043`.
- Browser/OS picker appearance, locale, momentum, or hit areas.
- A cylinder or other 3D wheel look: under a finger each sheet wheel's rows lie
  flat, and under a mouse the scrolling column keeps its tilt (DEC-2).

### Allowed variation

- **AV1 — Field presentation.** Label visibility, description, required/optional
  indicators, status placement, size, explicit width, and supported InputGroup
  composition may vary without changing the selected date.
- **AV2 — Date reachability.** Bounds, custom predicates, week start, month count,
  placeholder, and committed display format may vary within their public domains.
- **AV3 — Surface selection.** A caller may force any supported surface or retain the
  adaptive default; the resulting date value and shared field semantics remain the
  same.
- **AV4 — Theme output.** Themes may restyle admitted DateInput targets while shared
  Field, Calendar, Popover, BottomSheet, Icon, and clear-button owners retain their
  own targets and semantics.

### Transformation and precedence order

- **ORD1 — Surface.** Resolve explicit `presentation`; otherwise map deprecated
  `nativePicker`; otherwise use `adaptive-native`; with a calendar other than
  `gregory` or `iso8601`, use `adaptive-bottom-sheet` in place of `native` and
  `adaptive-native` (FR21); then resolve adaptive values from the primary
  pointer.
- **ORD2 — Display.** While editing, display pending typed text; otherwise format the
  controlled or optimistic ISO value with the selected format, provider locale, and
  resolved calendar.
- **ORD3 — Commit.** Parse or receive the proposed date, reject it when constrained,
  call `onChange`, then start `changeAction` with the same proposed value and busy
  presentation. Typed text proposes a date while typing only when it is complete,
  and on Enter or blur otherwise (FR20).
- **ORD4 — Clear.** Emit `undefined` through ORD3, then apply the selected surface's
  shipped focus policy: pointer and bottom-sheet fields reclaim focus, while the native
  field does not reopen the OS picker.

### Performance and resources

- **PR1 — Bounded calendar window.** The bottom-sheet calendar mounts a bounded window
  of month panes around the visible month rather than the complete reachable century.
- **PR2 — Scroll and gesture work.** On the native month scroller, scroll-driven
  measurement and selection updates remain animation-frame throttled or
  settle-driven. On the month pager under a finger, a drag moves the month grid
  without a render, and the visible month is reported once per half-month
  crossed, during the drag and the settle that ends it. Listeners, frames,
  timers, and observers are cleaned up on deactivation or unmount.
  Implementation gap: under a finger the touch calendar moves through the native
  month scroller; it has no drag-driven month pager.

## Accessibility contract

- **AR1 — Field relationships.** Every surface keeps an accessible label; required,
  description, status, disabled-reason, and busy relationships remain programmatically
  exposed through the shared Field contract.
- **AR2 — Pointer combobox.** The typable field exposes combobox state and a dialog
  popup; ArrowDown and Alt+ArrowDown open it, Escape dismisses it outside active IME
  composition, and focus is not reclaimed from an intentional destination.
- **AR3 — Validation.** Invalid typed or native selections are exposed without color
  alone; rejected typed input uses an assertive alert and native rejection remains
  described to assistive technology. Unreadable text, a date the calendar does not
  have, and an out-of-range date each announce a different catalog message
  (`spec:AST-111` AR3); the last two also show it as the field's error status,
  in place of a caller `status` while the rejected text stays.
- **AR4 — Disabled and busy focus.** A disabled or busy field remains focusable and
  exposes its state on the focused control (`spec:AST-085/FR1, FR5`); a disabled
  reason, when given, describes the field's focusable controls while all value
  mutation and picker activation stay blocked.
- **AR5 — Native control.** The browser/OS date input remains the labelled value owner;
  the painted committed-value overlay is excluded from the accessibility tree.
- **AR6 — Touch calendar.** The bottom-sheet Calendar preserves one reachable day per
  month, arrow-key date movement, named weekday columns, selected/today semantics,
  single-tab-stop wheels, and exactly one reachable footer action for the visible
  surface.
- **AR7 — Directionality.** DateInput's own calendar and clear controls exchange logical
  inline sides in RTL while preserving their labels and meanings; Calendar navigation
  remains owned and verified by `component:Calendar`.
- **AR8 — The month scroller is an APG grid** (`spec:AST-020/FR15`), as one
  composite control: each mounted month pane of the touch calendar's scroller
  is a `role="grid"` named for its month, with rows of `role="gridcell"` days,
  at most one day per pane holding a Tab stop,
  `aria-selected` on the chosen day; ArrowLeft and ArrowRight move by a day
  following the reading direction, ArrowUp and ArrowDown by a week, Home and End
  to the ends of the week, PageUp and PageDown by a month, and Enter or Space
  picks the focused day. Named exception: Shift+PageUp and Shift+PageDown move
  by a year, as in the APG date picker dialog. Implementation gap: PageUp and
  PageDown do not page by a month, and Shift+PageUp and Shift+PageDown do not
  move by a year.
- **AR9 — The field names its calendar** (`spec:AST-111` AR2). The pointer
  field's accessible description names the calendar only when the field's
  resolved calendar differs from the provider's resolved calendar, else
  `gregory`; `gregory` and `iso8601` count as one calendar. Both are compared after the Gregorian fallback for a runtime
  without `Temporal`, so a calendar that falls back compares as `gregory`. With a
  calendar other than `gregory` or `iso8601` and no caller `placeholder`, the
  empty focused field shows an example date in the calendar's numeric pattern as
  its placeholder; a caller `placeholder` shows in every state and replaces the
  hint. The example date is today when the field accepts it, else the nearest
  date it accepts.

### `spec:AST-013` FR12 exception — calendars that need `Temporal`

- **Capability:** typed entry, committed text, and the bottom sheet in calendars
  other than the Gregorian, Buddhist, Minguo, and Japanese calendars,
  which need the runtime's `Temporal`.
- **Reduction:** where `Temporal` is missing, as in Safari on macOS and iOS, the
  field reads, writes, and shows Gregorian dates, its presentations resolve as
  they do for `gregory`, and it warns once in development, naming a polyfill
  (`spec:AST-111` FR5). An application that loads a `Temporal` polyfill gets the
  full calendar. When a server and a browser use implementations of the Chinese
  or Dangi calendar that disagree on a month start, React renders the differing
  text again on the client (`spec:AST-111` FR19).
- **Preserved obligations:** `spec:AST-013` FR7 and FR8, and FR9 outside that
  case of the Chinese and Dangi calendars. The Gregorian field keeps every
  interaction, label, and announcement, and Buddhist, Minguo, and Japanese entry
  and bottom sheets work without `Temporal`.
- **Evidence:** real Chromium and Firefox typing in Persian, Hebrew, Japanese,
  and Buddhist fields with native `Temporal`, with `Temporal` removed and a
  polyfill loaded, and with `Temporal` removed and no polyfill; the Persian
  bottom sheet, the Hebrew wheels in a leap year, and the Japanese era wheel in
  real Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit in the same three modes.
- **Owner:** `nynexman4464`.
- **Review condition:** Safari ships `Temporal`.

## Current-state impact

- **Caller handlers while disabled (FR12).** Caller key, paste, and pointer
  handlers run on a disabled field on every surface; only `onClick` is skipped,
  against `spec:AST-085` FR2.
- **Fixed field height (FR10).** The field holds the shared element height
  instead of growing with a set text size, against `spec:AST-102` FR4.
- **Clear control under focus (FR11).** A clear control that unmounts while
  focused drops focus to the page, against `spec:AST-085` FR4.
- **Selection tick haptics.** Under a finger each row that reaches the band
  ticks with a short vibration where the browser has the Vibration API, as on
  Android. iOS Safari has none, so the tick is silent there (`spec:AST-094`
  FR23).
- **Month paging (AR8).** PageUp and PageDown do not page the touch calendar by
  a month, and Shift+PageUp and Shift+PageDown do not move it by a year, as AR8
  states they do.
- **Month pager (PR2).** Under a finger the touch calendar moves through the
  native month scroller rather than a drag-driven pager.

## Decision log

The governing presentation decisions are `spec:AST-043/DEC-1` through `DEC-3`.

### DEC-1 — A wheel resting on a disabled row continues to the nearest enabled row

**Reference:** `component:DateInput/DEC-1`
**Decider:** cixzhang, 2026-10-25

The bottom-sheet picker wheel rests as UIDatePicker's does: a wheel that stops on
a disabled row (outside `min`/`max`) continues to the nearest enabled row, a tie
going toward the committed row, and selects it there. It applies wherever the
wheel is rendered: DateInput's month and year wheels and the wheels of
DateTimeInput and TimeInput. Opening a bounded sheet never changes an untouched
value: a controlled value outside the bounds stays, and an empty field stays
empty.

Rejected: returning to the previous row, which makes the person scroll again to
reach the bound.

### DEC-2 — The sheet wheels' rows lie flat under a finger

**Reference:** `component:DateInput/DEC-2`
**Decider:** cixzhang, 2026-11-01

Under a finger, each sheet wheel in DateInput, TimeInput, and DateTimeInput lays
its rows flat at the scrolling column's pitch and fades them toward its edges,
without tipping or shrinking. The fade stops at 70% opacity at the edges, so
every row in view meets WCAG AA contrast in every shipped theme. Under a mouse,
the scrolling column tips and fades its rows on a scroll timeline.

Rejected: a UIPickerView-style cylinder look.

### DEC-3 — The minute wheel loops, and passing its seam keeps the hour

**Reference:** `component:DateInput/DEC-3`
**Decider:** cixzhang, 2026-11-10

Under a finger, the sheet's minute wheel in TimeInput and DateTimeInput loops:
past 59 comes 00, and before 00 comes 59. Passing the seam keeps the hour and
the AM/PM column, during the spin and at rest, as UIDatePicker's minute wheel
does on the iOS 26.2 simulator. The decider's ruling: "WH ios".

Rejected: carrying the hour at the seam, at rest or live during the spin, which
UIDatePicker never does.
