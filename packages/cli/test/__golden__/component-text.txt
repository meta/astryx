package: @acme/kit

# AcmeCard

A zorblax card that groups one topic.

**Import:** `import {AcmeCard} from '@acme/kit';`

## Best Practices

Do:
- Keep one idea per card.

Don't:
- Do not nest cards.

## Props

- `title`: `string` **(required)** - Heading text.
- `tone`: `'calm' | 'loud'` = `'calm'` - Visual weight.
