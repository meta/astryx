package: @acme/kit

# AcmeCard

A zorblax card that groups one topic.

**Import:** `import {AcmeCard} from '@acme/kit';`

## Best Practices

- **Do:** Keep one idea per card.
- **Don't:** Do not nest cards.

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
|------|------|---------|-------------|
| `title` | `string` | - | Heading text. **(required)** |
| `tone` | `'calm' \| 'loud'` | `'calm'` | Visual weight. |
