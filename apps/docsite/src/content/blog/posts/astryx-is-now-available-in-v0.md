Astryx is now available in [v0](https://v0.app/design-systems).

In v0, a design system is an agent skill. Builders can choose Astryx for a project, bringing its guidance, components, examples, and templates into context as v0 builds.

This collaboration with Vercel is one way we are exploring what it means for a design system to support both people and agents, and how system knowledge can remain useful at the moment of creation.
