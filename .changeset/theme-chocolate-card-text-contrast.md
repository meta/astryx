[fix] In light mode, secondary text and icons, links on the cool tinted cards, green, orange, red and yellow category text, and code comments and operators take deeper stops of their own colours, so they meet WCAG AA (4.5:1). Secondary text on the blue, purple, red and teal cards, and in dark mode on the green, teal and yellow cards, takes the next stop, so it also reads on the accent tint of a selected row or a numbered citation there, on the page and on a surface. Each value is a stop of the palette generator's ramp grown from the earlier colour.
