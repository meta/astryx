[feat] On a phone or tablet, drag a Lightbox photo down to close the viewer, as in the iOS Photos viewer. The photo follows your finger and shrinks a little as you pull, while the backdrop fades and the controls fade faster. The backdrop fades without its blur, which comes back once it is at rest. Let go moving down, however short the pull, and the photo carries on down as the viewer closes. Flick it back up, or bring it back to where you started, and it springs back into place. A zoomed photo still pans when you drag it; the drag to close starts from the fitted size. Videos are unchanged.

The viewer's controls now sit in their own layers, which fade together during the drag.
