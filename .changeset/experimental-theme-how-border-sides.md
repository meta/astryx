[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` reads per-side borders as said: colors written one per side (`#eee #eee #ddd`, or `rgb()` and `hsl()` colors) with a width or style beside them, said as a `border-color`, or on a one-mode theme are one `border-color` with every side as written, for the light mode only when any side is light, as one light border is (a light run said alone on a two-mode theme stays one light border, `border '1px solid light-dark(#eee, …)'`, as before), unless the answer also writes the line's width or style on a part with looks that draw no border of their own (buttons, tabs, most card looks): then the colors go to both modes, since those looks have no dark-mode border to keep, and the answer gives their contrast on the dark page; a border said on several sides ("a #ddd border on top and sides", "on top, left and right", "on the top and the bottom", "only a top and bottom border", "no border on the sides", "a darker top and bottom edge") is written on each side said, never only the first; a side said transparent ("with a transparent bottom", "a transparent bottom border") is that side's line drawn see-through, never a clear fill, while a graded "transparent" ("slightly", "barely") stays a fainter border and "on top of" stays a place; and one side's border said after a part's border ("input border #ddd, bottom border #999") is that part's side, never the theme's border color. Each of these answers says what it read ("read \"transparent\" as that side's line").
