[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: when an answer fills the page, surfaces and cards with one color, text a part draws on a see-through fill of its own over that color (a secondary selector's light wash), or in a color other parts also fill or edge with (a destructive menu item's red), is measured on what it sits on. Where it would not read at 4.5:1, the answer writes the nearest shade that does on that part only (`--on <part>`, with `--when variant:<look>` for a fill one variant draws), so the shared color keeps its other uses, such as the error edge of an invalid input.
