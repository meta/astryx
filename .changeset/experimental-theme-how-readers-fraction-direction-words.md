[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`:

- A fraction is read the way it's said, before or after the direction and in words or figures: "two thirds fainter", "fainter by a third", "1/3 fainter", "a fifth fainter", "a third more transparent". A fill made "20% more transparent" keeps 80% of its color.
- A color named after another part with its status or variant ("the error banner color", "the success badge color", "the color of the muted card", "the color of the destructive button") uses that version's color and changes the subject in full.
- A base theme's own style for a variant counts first: butter's transparent secondary button shows as transparent. A transparent fill used as a color gives the color it draws next (its outline).
