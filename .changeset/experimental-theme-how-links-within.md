[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` reads more of each ask:

- **Links in a container:** "links on cards", in a mode pair too, sets the token a link reads on the card, never app-wide. Before, one half of the pair changed card text instead of links.
- **Lookups:** every lookup is a command that runs.

Other readings:

- **Removals:** in "the shadow from dialogs and popovers", the items after the last "from" join it.
- **"Give neither … a thing":** "give neither the cards nor the popovers a border" removes the border from both.
- **Compound excepts:** "except the disabled secondary ones" keeps that variant in that state only.
- **An ask after an except:** "taller except the large ones and bolder" answers both asks, for every part of the ask.
- **Lists matched to a reference:** "the header and the sidebar the same color as the page" matches each item to the page.
- **An except said before its ask:** "all text except headings red" reads as one ask with its except.
- **Steps that change nothing:** a step that changes nothing is left out of an in-order sequence and named there. The rest still runs.
- **Step counts:** "2 steps rounder" stays two steps in the sequence.
- **Keeps after a comma:** "…, keep h1 and h2" is a keep.
- **Shadow excepts:** "remove every shadow except on cards" keeps the shadow tokens on the card, so elevated cards keep their shadow.
- **A color in a keep:** "keep disabled ones gray" names the look they keep, not a variant.
- **Excepts in one mode:** "except in dark mode" stays with its own clause, and "except the dialog in dark mode" leaves the dialog out in dark mode only.
- **Prohibitions of a look:** "never use rounded corners on buttons" before an ask keeps the buttons.
- **Tone words for text:** "card text dark in light mode and light in dark mode" reads dark and light as colors.
