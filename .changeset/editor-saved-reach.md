[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor`: a saved setting whose recorded reach grows is named once by the next change. The build records where each setting that reaches past its own part reaches, and the first change after that reach grows says so in its first line, with the command that keeps the setting on its own part. Settings saved before this release are recorded at their next build. `set … --only` on a setting the theme already holds replaces it, so the look leaves the group.
