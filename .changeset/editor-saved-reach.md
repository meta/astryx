[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor`: a saved setting never reaches more parts without a word. The build records where each setting that reaches past its own part reaches, and the first change after that reach grows says so at the top of its result, with the line that keeps the setting on its own part. `set … --only` on a setting the theme already holds replaces it, so the look leaves the group.
