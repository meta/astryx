[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: a feel word said for the whole theme now moves the dials whose help gives that feel, and each line says what it read. For example, "playful minimal recipe site" gives playful → corners round and vibrancy more, and minimal → spacing roomy and surfaces flat. The corners, spacing, surfaces, vibrancy and type help now says which feel each rung gives (see `how corners`). Words no help names are listed as not taken. A dial the ask sets itself keeps its value, and a feel word said of one part stays with that part.
