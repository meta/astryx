[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor`: a mistyped `--like` or `base` suggests only a base the app has installed, and with none near, `base none` and the installed bases. `undo <theme> base` or `mode` after a change and its reverse says it is back at the value the theme was made with. `new` refuses an option it does not take with its help, like the other editor commands. A theme folder copied or renamed by hand names the files to rename. A padding (or other) shorthand set after its sides on a part removes them, as its note says, and `undo` brings them back. A hand-edited part property value its CSS grammar refuses is named, like a dial's. `show` builds again when an output is missing or was edited by hand, as `compile --check` finds them.

When your own `<theme>.theme.ts` does not parse, `set`, `undo` and `show` refuse before writing or removing anything (the palette files under `tokens/` stay), with `cat` of the file; a failed change that removed the `tokens/` folder puts it back. A build temp file left by a command stopped mid-build is swept after ten minutes.
