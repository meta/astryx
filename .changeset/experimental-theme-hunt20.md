[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor`: a bare number on a length (`padding 4`) suggests `4px` with every flag kept, never the property's reset value. A part named by its class (`.astryx-card`) or its component (`<Card>`) suggests the part. What the buttons and surfaces dials write is read from the build, so an exact property under them is named in `set` and `show`, and undoing it names the dial that applies. An unknown state suggests the one meant (`pressed` is active), or how to see the part's states. After a change and its undo, the sheet no longer says it is out of date.
