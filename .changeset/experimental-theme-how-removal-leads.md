[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` reads every removal lead ("no", "without", "remove the", "drop", "get rid of" …) from one shared list: "without shadows and rounder corners on cards" removes the card's shadow and makes its corners rounder, never every shadow in the theme, and "remove the border and bigger padding on cards" gives the cards more padding. A typed mode word such as "constructor" is never read as a mode.
