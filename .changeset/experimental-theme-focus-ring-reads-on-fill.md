[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor`: a part the theme fills (a colored top bar) points the focus ring of what it holds at a color that reads on that fill, its own text color first, so keyboard focus on a top nav item still shows when the ring color matches the fill. A card or other surface inside it gets a ring that reads on its own fill again, and each is decided from the fill in effect in each mode. A focus ring color the theme sets on a part stays as set.
