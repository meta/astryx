[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: a color word is the theme's own shade where the theme has a family for it. Grey (gray, neutral) is a shade of the theme's grays, which every theme has, and another hue a shade of the palette family of that name; the shade is the one nearest the word's usual color, in each mode. A hue the theme has no family for, or a word with a shade before it ("light grey"), keeps its usual color.
