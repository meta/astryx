[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: a warning, error or success banner made darker or lighter steps its own status's colors, never the info blue. "Less visible" and "half as visible" borders are fainter, by the share named. "Less blurry" shadows are sharper (blur halved), never fainter. Questions about the shadows, the text weight, the background or how strong the borders are read the theme's value and change nothing. "Make the color of the text darker" is about the text. "The danger color brighter" makes it lighter. "The text 50% more transparent" scales the theme's text colors. "The page less dense" is the spacing dial. "The h3 size 20px" sets that heading level's size.
