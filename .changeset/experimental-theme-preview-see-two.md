[experimental] `astryx experimental theme`: going back to the preview's bare URL shows its first page, as a reload does. `editor undo` points its `see` line at the page about what it reverted. A preview whose URL depends on the app's routing, or whose folder was renamed by hand, is named where it is, by `see` and by `preview list`, instead of suggesting a new one. A pasted label in a theme name (`theme: t`, `name: t`) names the theme t, for `--theme` and for every editor command.
