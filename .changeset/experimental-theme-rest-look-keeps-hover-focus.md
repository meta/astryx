[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor` keeps a part's hover and keyboard focus visible when a setting changes its resting look: a resting background shorthand gives the part's hover and press Core's layer back; Core's focus and hover rings are drawn over a resting shadow, so a bevel never covers them; and a resting outline gives the part's focus state Core's focus outline back. A state the theme sets itself keeps what it set.
