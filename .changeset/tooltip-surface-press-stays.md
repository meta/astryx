[fix] A click inside an open Tooltip or HoverCard no longer activates a clickable container around its trigger. Clicking a FileInput status tooltip's text no longer opens the file picker, and clicking a tooltip or hover card inside a ClickableCard, an Item, TreeList or Table row no longer activates the card or row. Links and buttons inside a HoverCard keep working.
