[feat] In a `List` with `hasDividers`, a touch press hides the dividers on either side of the pressed row, so the pressed row reads as one unbroken highlight; both come back the moment the finger lifts, while the highlight fades. This applies under any finger (a coarse primary pointer); with a mouse the dividers stay drawn.
