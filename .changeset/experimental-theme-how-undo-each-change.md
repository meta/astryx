[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: each "the … change" said after an undo that names a part of its own and no value or mode, with no mode on the undo ("Undo the heading change and the card corners change"), is an undo of its own. It takes back that part's change said earlier in the ask; with none, it takes back what the theme saves for it, or says it sets nothing; it never asks for a change. Before, the second phrase was written as a new change, or the undo took nothing back. "too" after such a phrase adds nothing.
