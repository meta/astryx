[experimental] In `astryx experimental theme editor how`, a list piece that is only a color, with no look or part word of its own ("big buttons, a sky blue, high contrast"), is the theme's. It no longer fills the button named in a piece before it. "buttons rounder and red" still reds the buttons, and a part's colon list ("badges: green, amber, red") still colors that part.
