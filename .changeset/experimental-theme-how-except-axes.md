[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` keeps excepts apart truly:

- **Excepts on another axis:** an except on another axis ("buttons blue except the small ones") says it cannot be kept apart. Before, it said there was nothing to keep, and the excepted buttons changed anyway.
- **Comma-led excepts:** an except set off by commas is kept, as one with no part named is.
- **Excepts after a list:** a part-less except after a list reaches every part of the list.
- **Kept parts of a group:** a part of a group kept from a one-mode or status step is pinned in each status and mode of that step.

Other readings:

- **Mode pairs:** a mode pair is one subject wherever its part is said ("the text darker in dark mode and lighter in light mode on cards").
- **Text already at its limit:** text that is already as light or as dark as it goes says so, instead of answering "no match".
- **Heading levels:** "keep h1" is a keep.
- **Neither/nor:** "neither a border nor a shadow" reads its articles.
- **Removals:** "from menus and from tooltips" takes the thing of the item before it.
- **Lists of parts:** every ask after a list of parts reaches each part.
- **A second ask with only its value:** "rounder and red" and "bold and red" are two asks on the same part.
- **Neither/nor with parts as the subject:** "neither cards nor dialogs should have shadows" removes the shadow from each part, and "… should be rounded" squares each one.
- **Excepting an item of a list:** an except naming one item of a list leaves that item out of the change.
- **Modes said first:** "in dark mode …, in light mode darker" answers both halves.
- **Indicators:** "indicators" are the check and radio indicators.
- **"Every" in removals:** "remove every shadow" reads as "all shadows".
