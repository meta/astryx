[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor`: every repair reads a typo of a typed value, setting, part, state or theme or token name the same way: within one letter for a word of five letters or fewer and two for a longer one, two letters swapped counting as one. So `border-style line` is no longer offered `none`, and `display flxe` is offered `flex`, never `flow`; `nnone` and `normall` are still corrected. The words of a `how` question keep their own spelling reading.
