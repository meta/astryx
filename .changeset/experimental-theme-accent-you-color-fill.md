[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor set <theme> accent <color>` keeps the color you gave as the link and accent text color wherever it reads at 4.5:1 on what that text sits on, in each mode its fill is your exact color; only where it does not do links move, to the nearest shade that reads, and the result says so with the ratio. Text on the fill stays white or near-black by contrast (button and badge labels are normal-size text, which needs 4.5:1, so white on iOS blue #007aff, at 4.02:1, is not picked), and the note now names how to keep the other one exactly.
