[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor show` draws every part of the sheet and its pictures whole: a drawn hover no longer applies a high-contrast (forced-colors) rule, so a selected nav item's hover keeps its color; a cell grows to what it holds instead of cutting variants at its bottom; a table, a code block and an app shell take the whole row; a looping animation (a skeleton, a pulsing dot) is drawn at rest; and a section or a form layout holds sample text instead of nothing.
