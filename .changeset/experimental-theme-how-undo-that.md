[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: an undo that names no part ("undo that", "undo all of it", "then take them away") takes back every change said before it in the same ask, even when one of those changes wrote nothing ("make cards glossy and buttons bold, then take them away" no longer writes the bold). An ask said after it still stands. With no earlier change in the ask, it answers as before.
