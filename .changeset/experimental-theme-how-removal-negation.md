[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` reads a removal in a clause from the same shared list of removal leads as its rewrites ("strip", "nix", "scrap", "without the" included): "strip shadows from cards" removes the card's shadow, "nix the shadow and rounder corners on cards" removes only the card's shadow and makes its corners rounder, and "no shadows and borders on cards" removes both, as the "without" form does.
