[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` reads more phrasings through one shared grammar:

- **Keeping a part as it is:** "keep cards as they are", "leave inputs alone" and "don't touch the dialogs" all use one keep grammar, whether they come beside an ask or alone.
- **Density words:** cramped, airy, dense and the other density words come from one table that every density reading uses.
- **A fill's color after "with":** "selected table rows tinted with the accent at low strength" gives the selected state's accent wash.

`set vibrancy` now counts a color by whether its shades change. A warm or cool gray (neutral #78716c) takes vibrancy steps. A pure gray is refused, and the message names the color and says why.
