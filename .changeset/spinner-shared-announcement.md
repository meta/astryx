[fix] A standalone Spinner used to mount `role="status"` already holding its name, so screen readers often said nothing when it appeared. It is now an indeterminate progress indicator (`role="progressbar"` with no `aria-valuenow`, named by its visible string label, its `aria-label` or a translated "Loading") that announces once through the shared polite channel when it appears after page load, reading its rendered text in order with stacked lines kept apart, or its accessible name (AST-020 FR15; AST-086 FR2, FR7, FR11–FR12; WCAG 2.2 4.1.3). BaseTypeahead, Typeahead and Tokenizer keep their search Spinner named and never live while they announce the search themselves, and app code silences a composed Spinner with `aria-hidden`. No app change is needed (AST-017 FR53): a Spinner given a `role` other than `progressbar` stays silent, as before. The lab EmojiPicker and Schedule now hide their loading rings and announce their own busy start once, and the Comments history dialog announces it inside the dialog (AST-086 FR6, FR8, FR14); their loading words stay readable. Every Spinner's indicator changes from `status` to `progressbar`, so a test that queries it by role changes, including the hidden rings inside Button, CheckboxInput, CheckboxList, CheckIndicator, ChatMessageList, ChatToolCalls, CommandPaletteInput, ComplexSelector, DateInput, DateRangeInput, DateTimeInput, DropdownMenu's sub-menus, FileInput, MultiSelector, Selector, Switch, TextArea, TextInput, Thumbnail and TimeInput, and lab's EmojiPicker, Comments and Schedule.
