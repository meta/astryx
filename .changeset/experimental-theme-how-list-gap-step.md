[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: "a little more space between list items" (more room between the items of a part, with no amount) steps up that part's own gap by a quarter of the gap it has now (`gap <value> --on list`), instead of only naming the gap. "less", an amount of the gap such as "double", or a gap of 0 still asks for a value; a length ("12px") is written as given.
