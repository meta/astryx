[feat] `createMenuPress(menu, options)` runs the menu press model on any element without React: the row under a release acts, the highlight follows a held pointer, a mouse press on `trigger` opens the menu, and a finger held on it opens it after the long-press delay. `update(options)` merges new options, which apply from the next event without ending a press in progress. `startBehavior: 'items'` starts a press only on an item, so other content in the menu keeps its own clicks. `destroy()` removes every listener it added. `useMenuPress(menuRef, options)` is its React form: `triggerRef` names the trigger, and there is nothing to spread. Its press exists only while the menu element is mounted, so a menu that opens on a press of its trigger keeps its element mounted while closed. Deprecated (DEP-0019), and removed in 0.8.0: the options form, `useMenuPress({menuRef, …})` with its `menuProps` and `triggerProps`, which still works as before; and `DropdownMenuTriggerProps.onPointerDown` and `onContextMenu`: forward `ref` to the control instead: the press listens for its press-down on the document, which an ancestor that stops the press-down hides. `astryx upgrade` moves calls to the ref form and drops redundant forwards of the two handlers. DropdownMenu, BreadcrumbItem, NavHeadingMenu, TopNavMenu and the SideNav and TopNav heading menus move to the ref form with no change in behavior. The press starts after the page's own press-down handlers, as a handler composed before it did, so a press-down that one of them stops, or cancels inside the menu, starts no press. One change follows: a DropdownMenu caller's `onPointerDown` now reaches the menu element, where the press used to replace it, and its `preventDefault()` keeps the press from starting.
