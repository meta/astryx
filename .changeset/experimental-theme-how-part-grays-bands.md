[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: for a bare "neutral grays" ask, what keeps its tint is judged by one limit everywhere. A ground or fill (any background token or fill, whole-theme or on a part) is tinted up to an OKLab chroma of 0.1, and any other gray up to 0.05; past that it is a color of its own and is not named. A cream muted fill on a part, a side border or an outline color set on a part are now named, and a strong page color the theme sets is no longer called a tint.
