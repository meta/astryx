[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: the readability note on a part said to work in light and dark mode measures only text that sits on the page. A part that draws its own fill ("buttons that work in light and dark mode should have white text") puts its text on that fill, so it gets no page note. After a modal ("must", "could", "would", "may"), a plural part ("headings that could work in light and dark mode") is still read for both modes; a singular one ("text that must work …") keeps its reading.
