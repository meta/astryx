[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` reads more of a look's words from the dials themselves:

- A feel word said of a part that a dial's help names now also moves that dial: "elegant serif headings" also gives `type editorial`.
- "large readable text" is `text-size large`, no longer one step.
- A color word that names the theme's own color ("navy" in a theme with a navy accent) takes that color's shade.
- The list of looks shown beside words no setting reads now names every dial.
- "crisp" no longer squares the corners.
