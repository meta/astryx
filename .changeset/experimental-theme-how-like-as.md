[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` reads "like" and "as" as a comparison only in comparison forms ("as round as buttons", "the same padding as cards", "rounded like buttons", "like the dialogs are"): "make components like dialogs and drawers rounder" changes those parts, and "use red as the button background" sets that value. A lighter or darker color role steps its dark half the same way from its own value, and the answer check holds colors (text and borders for contrast in dark mode) and sizes to the asked way in both modes. "A light tint of the accent" is the accent mixed with transparent, never the full accent.
