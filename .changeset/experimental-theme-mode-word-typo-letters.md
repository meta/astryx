[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor`: a mode word typed with a typo that keeps its letters (`drak`, `darkk`, `ligth`) is that mode wherever a person types one, by one reader: a pasted label in a `how` question (`drak: cards #1c1c1e` changes the dark cards only) or in a `set` value (`'drak: #eeeeee'` offers the `--mode dark` line) and the `--mode` flag. A word of its own (right, tight, bark) is never read as a mode.
