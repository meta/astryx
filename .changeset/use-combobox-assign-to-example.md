[docs] A new `useCombobox — Assign To Field` example block spreads the field, listbox and option props from `useCombobox` onto a `TextInput` and a list of `Item` rows: typing filters a list of teammates, ArrowUp and ArrowDown move the active option while focus stays in the input, Enter or a press picks one, and Escape closes the list. Copy it with `astryx template useComboboxHookUsage <path>`.
