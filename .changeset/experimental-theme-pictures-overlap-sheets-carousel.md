[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor show --png` names each sheet whose pictures draw text over the text beside it, the family pictures included, and counts letters that reach a fraction of a pixel into their neighbor's text. Its sheets also widen a cell whose row scrolls sideways, so a carousel's picture shows every slide and cuts none at its edge.
