[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`:

- "the card's fainter text red" colors the cards' secondary text. A color after an ask on a part's text applies to that text ("the card text bigger and red"), and "fainter and red" gives a faded red.
- A word for a look before a part ("the borderless buttons", "the text-only buttons") means the button variants drawn that way, not a border to remove.
- "the destructive button the primary button color" uses the primary button's fill.
- "the dialog title red" colors only the title, not the subtitle.
- "a third darker", "darker by a third" and "half as dark" are read as the amount said, and "a third as strong" shadows are labeled softer.
- "the input's error border" means the error version of the input.
