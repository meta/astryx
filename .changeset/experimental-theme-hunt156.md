[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor show` shows a look's overlays open and names the parts the look leaves unchanged:

- The sheet's overlays section draws each tooltip, popover and hover card open beside its trigger, each drawer, bottom sheet and lightbox at its cell's size, and, open, each part another section holds whose control opens a menu or list (a dropdown menu, a selector, a tab menu), in light and dark, so its pictures show how a look styles them.
- `show` names, as `unreached`, the parts with a fill of their own the theme leaves on its base's look: no setting is on them, and their fill or text color is still the base's (for example toast, banner, badge).
- The author-a-theme guide opens with the theme editor, and a search for brand colors finds the theme topic.
