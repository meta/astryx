[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` reads a bare "neutral grays" ask as grays with no hue of their own. The ask is a clause that is exactly "neutral grays", "neutral greys" or "untinted grays" (after a lead verb such as "use"), said beside clauses that each name a part, a dial or a value and give no look to what the grays make (a hue, a warm, cool or ground tone, the neutral color, or a page, surfaces or background described by more than a dial's value: "neutral grays, warm surfaces" or "neutral grays, a golden page" keep their reading), in an ask that turns nothing away (a removal or dislike lead such as remove, get rid of, ditch, skip, I hate or avoid in the grays' own clause, or a prohibition word anywhere such as "don't", "never" or "shouldn't" (with a straight or curly apostrophe), keeps the earlier answer; a removal in another clause, "remove the card borders, neutral grays", or "drop shadows" as a thing does not), on a theme that sets no color in a part rule and no page, surface or card color of its own, and whose base keeps no tinted gray in a part rule. The clause is marked as it is read, never rewritten, so every other ask, the same words typed inside a longer phrase included, reads as before. The answer is judged by building the theme with Neutral's own mid gray, `#777777`, as the neutral seed: when that seed takes a tint out of the theme's grays (a warm or cool gray), it is offered, and the page, surface, text and border grays it makes lose their tint in every mode the theme has. When the grays already have none, the answer says so and writes nothing; a text or border gray the theme sets itself is named as keeping its tint whatever the seed is. Before, "neutral grays" only explained the neutral color.
