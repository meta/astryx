[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: a part's value "equal to" (or "identical to") another part's takes that part's value, as "the same as" does, and says so when that part has none to read. "Make the text 4.5:1 against the page" moves the text until it measures that ratio against the page, which is never changed (links too); text that measures it already is kept. "Never taller than 40px" is a ceiling, like "no taller than". A color bound ("darker but not darker than #1e3a8a") holds every color the answer writes, mode by mode, or says the part is past it already; it is never read as the color asked for. A table's lines and padding are read on its cells: a border ratio sets the token they read on the table, and "how dense is the table" answers with their padding. "What is the shadow" lists the shared shadow steps.
