[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: "progress bars" said in the plural is the bar's fill again, as in the singular ("progress bars white" fills the bar, never its outer box). In a part's colon list, an item that names the part's own piece by a word the word table reads with the part's name ("progress fill", "progress indicator"), and names no other part, is that piece: "progress bar: white fill on a dark track" and "progress bars: white indicator" now fill the bar (before, the track or also the checkboxes' indicators). An item that names a part of its own ("gray progress bar track", "white menu items"), or a word that already names a group holding the part ("checkboxes: white indicator"), reads as it did.
