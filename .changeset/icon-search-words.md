[experimental] `astryx experimental icon` answers a query of several words that no icon matches as a whole, such as a glyph named in a phrase with the control around it. Icons that carry some of its words come back as word matches (score 20), the icon carrying the rarest words first; a word no icon carries counts for nothing. A query that already had an answer keeps it.
