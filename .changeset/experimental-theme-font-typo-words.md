[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor set … font`: a weight or style word typed with a typo after a family the editor knows (`Georgia Blod`, `Inter semibld`, `Courier New Itallic`) is read as that word and refused with the family and weight lines and the name kept as typed, never saved as a family of that name. A typo of a font preset inside a list (`Robto, sans-serif`) is read as the preset, as it is alone.
