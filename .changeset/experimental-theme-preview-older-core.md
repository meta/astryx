[experimental] `astryx experimental theme`: with an older installed Core whose component docs have no playground defaults, the catalog now builds each component's demo from its doc's props. Required props get a sample, and a group gets three of the items its doc suggests. The contact sheet, the Components page, `how` answers and contrast checks keep every component. A part with its own doc, such as Heading, now shows on its own. An open overlay shows once on the pages people see, and a switched-off switch or checkbox is labeled for the state it shows. `preview list` marks a theme whose folder is gone (`themeMissing`). After a damaged step record, a page file in no record is named, never deleted. `--png` with a browser that is installed but fails now names the error.
