[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor`: an effect (a text or box shadow, a filter) set on one part stays on that part. It reaches the other members of its family role only when `--on` names the group (`--on selected-items`, `--on button-like`). The always-alike groups (inputs, checks, menus) still take every change as one, and an overlay's box shadow still reaches the other overlays.
