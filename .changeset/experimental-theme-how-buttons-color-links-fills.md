[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`:

- "the buttons the color of the links" fills the buttons with the link color, instead of turning their text the accent color.
- A part's fainter or muted text, said with an owner ("the card's muted text") or a place, is that part's secondary text. "the card's fainter text lighter" changes how light that text is, not its transparency.
- "the row hovered and selected" means the row in both states at once; neither state is dropped.
