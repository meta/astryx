[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor`: its phone rule repeats only your exact font settings, and the notes say what phones show (on top of a base theme, phones show the base theme's type for fonts your file sets, a Core limit). A `[light, dark]` pair your theme file gives a token that is not a color (a font, a size, a weight) is refused by name, as `--mode` is on those tokens, and a font your file sets through `typography` is seen as the file's.
