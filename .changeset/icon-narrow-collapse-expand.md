[experimental] Icon definitions `collapse` and `expand` now mean only making something smaller or larger. Full screen has its own definitions, `fullScreen` and `exitFullScreen`, so `collapse` no longer says "exit full screen" and `expand` no longer says "full screen" or lists the word `fullscreen`. An ask for full screen answers with `fullScreen` first, and with an icon set, with the set's full-screen icon.
