[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: one bare look taken away by a pronoun after a change that starts with make, set, use, give, add, change or turn, joined before it by "and", "but" or "then" ("make the buttons blue and the border is ugly, hide it"), is never that change's part: nothing is written for it, and the answer asks which part. A look that points back to the change's part with "their", "its", "on it" or "around it" ("their border", "the border on it") is read the same way: nothing is written for it, and the answer asks which part; "the border on them" reads as before. Other forms (two looks, a verb later in the clause, another verb, a comma, a new line, "!", a question before the ask, or a look said with other words) keep the reading they have.
