[feat] Under a coarse primary pointer, as on a phone or a tablet, the picker wheels in the TimeInput, DateTimeInput and DateInput bottom sheets now move like UIPickerView. Their rows keep the 28 px pitch and lie flat, fading toward the edges of the column in every browser; the scrolling column under a mouse tilts and fades its rows only where the browser supports scroll-driven animations, and shows a plain list elsewhere. The row under the band is in semibold accent text on its plate while the wheel rests and while a finger drags it; no row is marked while the wheel coasts, snaps or turns. A flick coasts at UIKit's deceleration onto the row UIKit's projection picks. A finger landing on a moving wheel stops it. A tap on a row turns it onto the band, and a value changed from outside while the sheet is open, as when a bound clamps the time, turns the wheel onto its row. Past the first or last row, a drag rubber-bands and the wheel bounces back. As on iOS, the value commits when the wheel comes to rest, and the coast keeps running while the page's main thread is busy. Arrow keys and clicks on a row still commit at once. Under reduced motion a flick still coasts, as scrolling does; a tap, a key or a slow release cross-fades onto its row. `presentation="popover"` keeps the previous surface; no value keeps the native-scroll column under a finger. Each row that reaches the band ticks with a short vibration where the browser supports it, as on Android; iOS Safari has no vibration. Under a fine primary pointer, as with a mouse on a desktop, the wheels keep the scrolling column. (#8159)
