[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` writes two kinds of color said once for the whole theme for both modes: a light border color takes its twin as far off the dark page (the theme's gray for a gray), and a link color the dark page leaves under 4.5:1 takes a twin that reads there (on the muted accent fill too, where it can). A plain "1px <color> borders" also gives a border to the surfaces, menus, buttons and tables Core draws with none (never to a badge, avatar, collapsible or the layout frame).
