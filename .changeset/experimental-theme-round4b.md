[experimental] `astryx experimental theme`: an accent or status fill keeps your exact color in dark mode only when it also stands out from the dark page (3:1) besides text reading on it; otherwise dark mode uses the nearest shade that does both, and the result says why. The preview's Colors page marks such a fill under the shade it really uses, for dark mode too. Suggestions from `preview add` quote the options they repeat, such as a `--path` with a space.
