[feat] A reorderable List row's handle is its 16px grip alone, with no box round it, so it takes the room a start icon takes. A mouse hits it anywhere in an invisible 24px square round the grip, the WCAG 2.5.8 minimum, where reorder mode's fill and the focus ring also sit; a finger keeps its 44px target. A handle that shows on hover now needs 28px outside the row's text, down from 30px.
