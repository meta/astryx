[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` guards its answers, so a phrasing it was never taught gives a named refusal instead of a wrong write.

**Guards:**

- **Direction:** an ask that makes something fainter, less visible or more see-through, or removes it, never adds or strengthens a border, outline, ring or shadow anywhere. A way asked of a line never adds one to a look Core draws without one. Each such step is left out and named.
- **Keeps:** every kept scope is held after the whole in-order sequence, and that sequence runs the except with its keeps.
  - A compound except keeps that variant in that state only.
  - An except's mode or state stays with its own clause.
  - Several excepts, each with its own mode, are each kept in that mode.
- **Questions:** a question with no clause that asks for a change never writes.
- **Unknown parts:** an unknown part is said, never read as the whole theme or given the part said before it.

**Readers the guards point to:**

- **Fainter borders:** a fainter border is its color at a lower alpha. A part that draws no border says so.
- **See-through text:** see-through text is the text colors at a lower alpha, never the page.
- **Focus rings:** a see-through focus ring is its color at a lower alpha.
- **Table row dividers:** thicker row dividers are the width token the cells read, set on the row.
- **Blur:** "blurrier" shadows have more blur, not more strength.
- **Removal lists:** the items in the middle of a removal list keep its thing.
- **Links in a place:** links in a place take the link token set on that place.
- **Outline buttons in a color:** outline buttons in a color get no fill, with that color as their border and text.
- **A held theme color:** a bound holds a theme color's dial and keeps a tint's alpha.
