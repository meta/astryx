[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor`: the font dial reads every weight or style word after a family, each through the one typo limit (`Inter Bold Itallic`, `Inter Tihn`), and offers the family and headings lines for them as for the exact words. A `new` refused for its name keeps the repair of a font the font readers refuse (`--font roboto` for `--font Robto`). The note on a family near a system font with a weight word (`Arail Bold`) names lines set takes: the family, and the headings weight.
