[experimental] Tokenizer gains a touch surface behind `experimental={{presentation: 'bottom-sheet' | 'adaptive'}}`. `bottom-sheet` replaces the inline combobox with a trigger that shows the selected tokens and opens one tall modal sheet: a search row on top, full-width checkbox rows that keep their place while the sheet is open and regroup selected-first on the next open, 50-row progressive rendering for long lists, a `Create "…"` row with a trailing Add action under `hasCreate`, and a footer with a confirmed Clear all and Done. `adaptive` uses the sheet on compact coarse-pointer screens and the popover otherwise, resolved the same way Selector resolves it. The sheet surface loads on demand, so a Tokenizer that does not opt in does not carry it. The trigger describes its selected tokens to assistive technology, announcements made while the sheet is open reach assistive technology through a live region inside the sheet, with `experimental` passed the component also accepts a `Ref<HTMLInputElement | HTMLButtonElement>` because `ref` reaches the trigger in the sheet presentation (the exported `TokenizerProps` interface is unchanged), and in development the field warns once about props that have no surface in the sheet presentation (`renderItem`, `renderToken`, `endContent`, `hasClear`, `hasEntriesOnFocus`, `menuWidth`, `tokenOverflowBehavior`, `hasAutoFocus`). New `@astryx.tokenizer.*` catalog keys for the sheet's strings; the sheet's parts expose no theming targets while the option is experimental. Anything inside Tokenizer's `experimental` prop may change or be removed in a patch release; omitting it keeps the stable behavior.
