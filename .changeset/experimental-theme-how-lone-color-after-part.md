[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: a list piece that is only a color, with no look or part word of its own ("big buttons, a sky blue, high contrast"), is the theme's accent when the part before it draws with the accent, such as a button or a checkbox, and the color has a hue. White, black or a gray there, which is never an accent, is that part's fill ("rounder buttons, white" fills the secondary and ghost buttons; "rounded checkboxes, white"). After a part that is only text, or one with a plain fill, the color is that part's text or fill ("bold headings, dark gray", "rounder cards, white"); after bare "labels", which names two parts, nothing is written. The color is never dropped. "buttons rounder and red" still reds the buttons, and a part's colon list ("badges: green, amber, red") still colors that part.
