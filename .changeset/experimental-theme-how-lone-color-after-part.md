[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: a list piece that is only a color, with no look or part word of its own, after a part that draws with the accent, such as a button or a checkbox, is the theme's accent when the color has a hue ("big buttons, a sky blue, high contrast", "rounded checkboxes, navy"). White, black or a gray there, which is never an accent, is that part's fill ("rounder buttons, white" fills the secondary and ghost buttons; "rounder buttons, dark gray"; "rounded checkboxes, white"). After a part that is only text, or one with a plain fill, the color is that part's text or fill ("bold headings, dark gray", "rounder cards, white"). After any other part ("bigger icons, red", "bigger tabs, blue") the color reads as before. "buttons rounder and red" still reds the buttons, and a part's colon list ("badges: green, amber, red") still colors that part.
