[feat] Toast takes `presentation`, on `ToastViewport` (every toast it shows) and on `Toast`: `'adaptive'` (the default), `'toast'` or `'notification'`. Under `'adaptive'`, a toast under a finger (a coarse primary pointer, on every touch device, iOS and Android alike) now follows iOS notification banners, as `'notification'` does on every device. The card stays still for the first 14 px of finger travel and then follows at 0.55 of the finger, with no jump. On release, a quick flick toward the edge dismisses even when it barely moved the card, as does a drag that leaves the card far enough out; a release moving back toward rest, or anything else, springs the card back. However a toast is dismissed (a swipe, the close button, the timeout, the app's dismiss), the card leaves as the banner does: it slides past its edge from where it is, continuing a flick's speed, without fading, and its row closes after it. While the card springs back or leaves, touches pass through to the page. Previously a toast under a finger followed Toast's own swipe, the card moving with the finger as it faded and shrank, and faded out when dismissed: `presentation="toast"` keeps that on every device. With a fine pointer the swipe and exit look as they did; a flick now reads the speed at the lift from the shared release filter instead of the drag's average, so a slow drag ending in a quick throw dismisses and a pen that rests before it lifts does not flick. Under reduced motion nothing slides, springs or drifts: on a touch screen the toast fades in place before its row closes, and with a mouse or keyboard it changes at once.
