[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: a look said and then taken away by a pronoun takes away only that look on that part ("code block copy button border is ugly, hide it", "the card's border is ugly, hide it", "the shadow on the cards is too strong, remove it"), never the part itself. Only an ask said on its own is read this way: one part, the look, and words that only judge it. A button named with its variant ("the primary button border is ugly, hide it") is that variant. A look said for a side, a state, a mode or another variant, for several parts, or in an ask joined to another by "and", "but" or "then", keeps the reading it has without the pronoun.
