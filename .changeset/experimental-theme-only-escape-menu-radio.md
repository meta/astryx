[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor set … --only` always works as an escape from a group: on a member of a group that spreads it keeps the change to that member, and on a part that is styled on its own already (a sub-part, a card) it is taken and said, in place of a refusal; only a group itself, which has no one member to keep to, refuses it. A menu's radio is kept apart from every other radio: "square corners but keep menu radios round" says its circle is left as it is, and a round ask on a menu radio the theme squared offers the undo that gives the circle back. A light-only theme reads `--mode light` as no `--mode`, as a dark-only theme reads `--mode dark`.
