[docs] A new `useImageMode — Photo Caption` example block shows a caption over a photo's sky. `useImageMode` samples only the strip under the caption, and `MediaTheme` gives the caption light or dark text from the result. A Beach photo switch swaps a night photo for a bright beach photo, and the caption keeps the dark fallback while the new photo loads. Copy it with `astryx template useImageModeHookUsage <path>`.
