[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor`: every picture `--png` takes carries the editor's stamp (a PNG text chunk naming the theme), so it is known as the editor's even without its record; a picture an earlier version took, with no record yet, is taken over when it is the editor's 1280-pixel window, and the result says so once. Only links among the files the editor reads or writes are refused, each named with where it points; a link to a file of yours (notes, a mockup) is left alone. A `new` refused for its name leaves out of its suggestion a vibrancy that would change nothing (no color given), and says so. The `--png` help of new, set, show and undo names the picture record and when no picture is taken.
