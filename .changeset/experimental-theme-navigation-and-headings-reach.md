[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: navigation and heading asks reach what they name. "Side navigation" and "top navigation" are those parts; a selection said of a part is its selected items ("white sidebar, blue selection"); a role word right before a written color names that color ("our brand color #1E3A8A"); a color and its part said right after another color and part is an item of its own; a top bar's text color reaches the top nav too (its items wherever it sits); and a heading's text color reaches every part Core draws as a heading element (an empty state's title, a markdown heading).
