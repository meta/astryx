[fix] Muted cards in dark mode draw the theme's own light text on their dark fill, and links on coloured cards take a deeper accent stop. Inside a coloured card, a nested card, a field, inline code, an open menu, a selected segment, an input group addon, a step number, a banner and a striped table row draw the theme's own text again, and a transparent card keeps the card's ink. Secondary text on a selected row, a dropzone or a numbered citation steps to the nearest stop that meets WCAG AA (4.5:1) on the accent tint over the page, a card and the muted fill; inside a coloured card, up to two coloured cards deep, it takes a dark stop like the rest of the card's ink.
