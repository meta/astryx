[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` never ends a sentence at the period of a bracketed "(e.g. …)" or "(i.e. …)", of "vs.", or of "etc." before a comma, a bracket or the end. "Header: navy. Cards: white background (e.g. snow), gray header." keeps the gray header on the cards. A bracketed "(e.g. …)" or "(i.e. …)" right after a part name ("the header (e.g. the top bar)") is an aside that changes neither the part nor what is set.
