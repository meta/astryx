[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` never ends a sentence at the period of an abbreviation ("e.g.", "i.e.", "vs.", and "etc." before a comma, a bracket or the end). "Header: navy. Cards: white background (e.g. snow), gray header." keeps the gray header on the cards. An example of the color just said, joined by a comma with "e.g.", "i.e.", "for example" or "such as" ("navy, e.g. a dark blue"), restates it and is never a second color for the part; a typed value in the example ("e.g. #fafafa") is kept as typed. A bracketed "(e.g. …)" or "(i.e. …)" right after a part name ("the header (e.g. the top bar)") is an aside that changes neither the part nor what is set.
