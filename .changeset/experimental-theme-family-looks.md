[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` and `set`: a look said of one member of a family (a gradient on buttons or menu rows, a frame of a border and a shadow on dialogs, a selected pill's corners and fill) goes on that member's family group (`button-like`, `option-rows`, `overlays`, `selected-items`), so every member that draws alike takes it; a plain fill, a shadow or border alone, a removal or a member said with a variant stays on that member. `set … --on <member>` stays exact and names the family's members that do not share the look, with the one line that gives it to the family.
