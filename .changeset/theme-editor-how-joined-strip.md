[experimental] `experimental theme editor how` answers "pagination pages in one bordered strip": the row takes the border and corners with no gap between its controls, each control a separator after it, the first and last control the row's corners just inside its border, the last no separator, and the ellipsis a separator too. Corners, a plain shadow, and a line color, width or dashed or dotted style said with it ("with 3px corners, a soft shadow and #dddddd lines") go on the strip; another look said with it (a large shadow, thick lines) is answered on its own. Said for one mode, the strip keeps its shape in both modes and only its line color takes the mode. It reads the parts themselves: the controls are the part whose ends Core marks first and last. "pagination pages" and "pagination cells" name the pagination's controls.
