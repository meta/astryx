[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: a "clear the …" (or "clear my …", "clear all …") that leads an ask and whose object is a removable thing takes it away, as "remove" does ("clear the dark mode card background" gives the card a transparent fill in dark mode). It stays a reset, as before, when the ask names the person's edits ("clear my border changes"), something someone made ("clear the border I added"), a way back ("back to the base value"), a color or a value, or a property of the thing ("the border color"), and wherever it does not say which; "clear" said as a quality ("a clear border") reads as before.
