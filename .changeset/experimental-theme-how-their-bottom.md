[experimental] In `astryx experimental theme editor how`, "their" and "its" point back to the part said last ("make dialogs wider, their titles bigger" sizes the dialogs' titles, not every heading). A color said of one side with no fill word ("hovered tabs with a #0969da bottom", "… and rgba(0,0,0,0.25) on the bottom") is that side's line, never the part's fill.
