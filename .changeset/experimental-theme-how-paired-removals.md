[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` reads a second item after a removal as a removal only when it carries its own removal word or is a plain removable noun ("no shadows and borders"); a noun with a comparative or an adjective is an ask of its own: "no shadow and rounder corners on cards" removes the card's shadow and makes its corners rounder, and "no border and bigger padding on cards" gives more padding, never none; the removal stays on the part named.
