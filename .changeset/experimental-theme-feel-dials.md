[experimental] The theme editor has two feel dials. `press` and `hover` each take darker, lighter, stronger, or a percent from 1% to 60%; neither takes none. A wash shows only where it differs from the fill under it: a white one on a white fill, or a black one on a black fill, shows nothing. Each sets the shared wash that the parts painting it (buttons, segmented items, tabs, list and tree rows, calendar days, and cards you click when pressed) draw over their own fill while pressed or hovered; a menu row's hover is its focus, which follows the dial too, so the look follows each part's colors in every variant and mode. The looks Core paints with the same wash at rest keep the theme's wash (an exact one it saves included): an open menu's trigger, a toggle that is on. A highlighted option follows the pointer, so it follows the dial. A part whose pressed or hover background the theme sets keeps the theme's wash in the mode it is set for, and a part that saves the wash itself keeps its own. A wash the theme's own source sets wins over the dial, which writes nothing for it. `set` refuses `--mode` on either dial; for darker, lighter or stronger it names the line that sets that mode's wash token, and for a percent it names the token. `how` writes that line for darker, lighter or stronger asked for one mode, and names the token for a percent. Parts that tint their own hover (a switch, a checkbox, a radio, a slider, a link) keep that tint. `how` reads "darker on press", "darker on click" and "8% tint on hover" as the dial; a border, text or shadow said with its moment stays that look's.
