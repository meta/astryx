[docs] A new `Written report` page template, not yet listed as ready: one written document read top to bottom, such as a decision record, a proposal, a brief or a memo. A header carries the status, the authors and share and export controls; a summary card holds the key facts; prose sections hold a quoted decision, a comparison table and numbered rollout steps; the rail holds a section outline, related documents and reviewers, and folds into an "On this page" picker on narrow screens. Copy it with `astryx template written-report <path>`.
