[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor set`: a hover background or a focus outline the theme sets for a wider scope (the whole part, or one variant) now stays under a resting setting for a narrower one (one variant, or a variant and a size). Before, the resting rule outweighed it by order, so small ghost buttons lost the theme's hover or focus ring.
