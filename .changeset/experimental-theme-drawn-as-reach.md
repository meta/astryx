[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor set`: a look on a part names the parts drawn as it only where it reaches them: a ghost button's look names the parts drawn as ghost buttons (a calendar's arrows, a code block's copy button, an input's clear button, a toggle button), each with its keep-plain line, and a primary button's look names none of them. A padding or other property a part draws through a variable of its own (a card's padding) names the parts drawn as it that read no such variable (a clickable card, a selectable card) as not reached, with no line, instead of counting them as reached with a keep line that would take their padding away.
