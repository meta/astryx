[docs] A new `useClickableContainer — Clickable Project Card` example block makes a project card clickable anywhere with `useClickableContainer`: a click on the card follows its title link, while the Star button, the owner link and a text selection keep their own behavior, and Ctrl/Cmd+click or middle-click opens the project in a new tab. Copy it with `astryx template useClickableContainerHookUsage <path>`.
