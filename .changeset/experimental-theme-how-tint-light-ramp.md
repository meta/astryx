[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: a color named by a tint word ("cards tinted blue", "selected nav items tinted forest green") fills with a light tint of it from the palette generator's ramp for it, at the stops Core's card tints use (pale in light mode, deep in dark mode). Text asked with it is written in the modes it reads in, and the answer names an ink for a mode it does not read in. A color said with its own shade word ("a light blue tint") and a color with no tint word are written as said, as before.
