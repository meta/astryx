[feat] Themes can reach four more parts. The default look does not change.

- A theme's `spinner` indicator replaces the ring a Spinner turns.
- A theme's `tree-disclosure` indicator replaces a TreeList row's rotating chevron with a mark for each state.
- NumberInput's stepper buttons carry `number-input-stepper` with `direction` (`increment` or `decrement`).
- The close buttons of Toast and Banner carry `toast-close` and `banner-close`, so a theme can style them apart from other buttons. Toast's close button draws in the toast's text color; Banner's keeps its status text color.
