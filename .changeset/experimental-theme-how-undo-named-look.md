[experimental] In `astryx experimental theme editor how`, an undo said alone that names a bare "size" or "weights" takes back only that look's earlier change ("Make the headings bigger. Make the headings bold. Undo the heading size change." takes back the sizes and keeps the bold), where it took back nothing before; when no change of that look was said, it says so. An undo followed by another "… change" phrase on a part that had a change earlier reads as before.
