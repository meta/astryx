[feat] `astryx doctor` warns about a Core button that does nothing when pressed (`button-actions`): a `Button` or `IconButton` from `@astryxdesign/core` with no `onClick`, `clickAction`, `href`, or `submit` or `reset` type, or with an empty handler or a link that goes nowhere (`#`, unless the app routes by the URL hash), where nothing around it acts on a press. The warning names each button's file, line and label. Doctor reads the app's own source as syntax trees and leaves out test, story and fixture files. It does not report a button it cannot judge: one with props spread in, a ref, a tooltip, or a disabled or loading state, or one inside a link, a trigger prop, a custom element, a `Provider` read off a namespace import of the app's own module, or a component it does not know, or one that holds an HTML control, a custom element, another package's component other than an icon, or content doctor cannot read (`{children}` a wrapper passes in). It does not judge a workspace member's buttons when the app imports the member by its package name. When doctor cannot follow a linked folder, it treats no component as a page. When the app's own code listens for a press through the DOM (`addEventListener('click', …)`, an `addEventListener` whose event a variable names, a library's `.on('click', …)`, or an `onclick` property), doctor judges no button and reports the check as not run. Nor does it judge a component that a module may load by a path doctor cannot read (a computed `import()` or `require()`, `import.meta.glob` or `require.context`, in a module, an MDX or Astro page, or a module that does not parse), or that an MDX or Astro page names. The check never fails `doctor`.
