[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: a hue said for one mode's page, by itself at the start of the ask, of a line or list item, or after a comma or period ("dark mode should be a deep dark plum", "light mode is pale blush", "dark mode goes near-black with a purple tint") is now that mode's page in the color's hue, at the page's own lightness there, so the page and the app shell's frame follow it. Before, "dark mode should be … dark plum" made the whole theme dark-only. A mid tone named for a mode's background (chocolate brown) takes the page's lightness with its hue kept, and a hue said for everything at the start of the ask or after a comma or period ("blush pink everything") is the page, where the reader took nothing from it before; a warm or cool look said for everything ("warm beige everything") stays the neutral's grays. A color already as readable as that mode's page is kept as said. A color said with a part, a role or a token ("dark mode gets a red accent", "light mode gets blue links"), one a "with", "and" or "but" goes on from with no comma before it ("light mode should be cream with blue buttons"), and "dark mode is gray" read as before.
