[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: "header(s)" or "title(s)" with a text word (bolder, bigger, font, weight, size, serif) means the headings' type, and with a surface word (background, bar, border, sticky, height) the app header part; a part that owns its header ("table header") keeps it. "just" and "only" keep the scope they name ("just the headers bolder" changes the headings, never the body text).
