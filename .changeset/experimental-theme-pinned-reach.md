[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor`: a saved setting's reach is pinned. A setting that reaches beyond its own part records the parts it reached when it was made (`reached` in the settings file, by the setting's identity), and the build, `show` and the previews keep it there. Parts its family gains later (an editor that widens a group, a member Core adds) are offered, never applied: the result says each once with the same line, which sets it again at its whole reach (`offered`), and `show` lists them in its notes. A setting saved with no record keeps today's reach and is recorded as it reaches.
