[experimental] theme editor how: in a part's colon list ("tabs: …", "tables: …"), every item belongs to the part named before the colon. A text size in "filled buttons: …" stays on the buttons instead of changing the whole theme. "outlined buttons" no longer turns a text size into a border. "the active page" in a pagination list is its current page button, and its white text stays on it. "on the rest" is the tabs that are not selected. "header background" in a table list is the table header, and a hover after it is the rows'. "the active page in pagination" now finds its current page button too.
