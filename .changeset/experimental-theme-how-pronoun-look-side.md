[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: one side of a border, said on its own and then taken away by a pronoun, goes from that side only ("the bottom border on the cards is ugly, hide it" writes no bottom border on cards, and the other sides keep theirs). This narrows the plain pronoun reading's "a side keeps the earlier reading" to sides of looks other than a border. A border side said with a state, a variant or a mode, or as a place ("the border at the bottom of the cards"), keeps the reading it has without the pronoun.
