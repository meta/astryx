[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how "glossy badges"` makes a gloss from each look's own fill instead of the one white-to-indigo sample gradient it wrote on every part named: a lighter top and a darker bottom around the fill. In each mode where the fill is an opaque theme token (a badge's color, a header's surface), the gloss is a live gradient over that token with `color-mix()`, so it follows whatever the theme or the same answer sets it to; a see-through fill is glossed in each mode at its own alpha, carried by the gloss alone, and a color said in the ask, or a literal fill the theme saved, is glossed as colors from the palette's tone scale, and only on looks with a resting fill of their own. Each look along the axis the part's fills vary by (a badge's variant, a toast's type) is glossed from its own fill. A gloss of a color said gets a readable-text line where its label would not read, unless the ask colors that text. A look with no resting fill of its own (also when a color is said), a gradient the theme set, a part that shows its hover and press as a wash over its fill (a button) or tints its own fill (a switch's track), a part that paints another fill for a state of a look it fills (a current pagination dot, a pressed toggle), a part whose looks draw colors the editor does not read (a banner's statuses), a color said for the whole of a part whose fills are its looks' ("glossy blue badges"), and a tinted color write nothing, and the answer says why.
