[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how "glossy buttons"` makes the gloss from the part's own fill in each mode instead of one sample indigo gradient on every part: the fill ten tones lighter at the top and ten tones darker at the bottom, through the fill (a white fill keeps white at the top, a black fill black at the bottom), written as its colors. The fill is one said for the part in the ask ("glossy blue buttons", "Top bar: dark (#222), glossy"; never a color said of its text or border), else the fill the theme sets on the part, else each variant's own fill; a variant, mode or part with no plain fill of its own keeps its look, so a top nav inside a bar writes nothing.
