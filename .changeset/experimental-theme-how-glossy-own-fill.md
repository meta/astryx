[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how "glossy buttons"` makes the gloss from the part's own fill in each mode: a lighter top and a darker bottom around that fill, written as its colors, instead of one sample indigo gradient on every part. A color said with it ("glossy blue buttons") is the fill it is made from; a part that draws no fill of its own, such as a top nav inside a bar, writes nothing.
