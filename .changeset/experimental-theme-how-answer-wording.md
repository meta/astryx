[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` says what it writes. The words an answer repeats no longer end on a bare "when" once a state is read, and part names take their plural ("switches", never "switchs"); a keep said first ("keep the switches and the background …") ends at a whole verb. A color said as how something looks now ("too bright blue", "is still gray") is never written as the value, and a color said against the one it replaces ("navy, not bright blue") is one ask with no line about the other. Two parts listed with a word describing the second ("the info banner and draft badge") share the ask. A near color off a pure one ("never pure white") takes the theme's own neutral. A one-line look keeps its page, text and font ("green text on black, monospace"), and "text on black" reads the color under the text as the page.
