[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how <picture.png>` measures the look's traits from the picture's pixels, not only its colors: control height (on the button and input families), corner radius, edge width, how panels sit, the accent fill, line spacing from a wrapped paragraph and a clearly regular heading weight, each answered with a checked command that says where it was read and from how many boxes, and written only where enough boxes agree and, for controls, where more than one kind of control shows (a crop of badges alone writes none and says why). What a picture cannot tell exactly (text size, a heavier heading weight, line spacing with no wrapped paragraph, the typeface) or no group setting makes yet (gradients, bevels, shadows on controls) is listed under `unsure` with options, never written, a value the theme sets itself keeps its value, and a picture over 8 million pixels or too grainy to hold boxes keeps its colors with its look named as not measured.
