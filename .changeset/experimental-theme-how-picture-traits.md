[feat] `astryx experimental theme editor how <picture.png>` measures the look's traits from the picture's pixels, not only its colors: control height, corner radius, edge width, how panels sit and the accent fill, each answered with a checked command that says where it was read and from how many boxes, and written only where enough boxes agree and, for controls, where more than one kind of control shows (a crop of badges alone writes none and says why). What a picture cannot tell exactly (text size, heading weight, leading, the typeface) or no group setting makes yet (gradients, bevels, shadows on controls) is listed under `unsure` with options, never written, and a value the theme already sets keeps its value.
