[docs] A new `usePressFeedback — Pressable Card` example block shows the hook on an Astryx Card: the hook's marker and the `interactionOverlayStyles.backgroundImage` overlay go straight on the Card, so pressing the card darkens it the way every Astryx pressable does, under a finger, a mouse or a key, and `focusOutlineStyles.focusVisible` gives keyboard users the shared Astryx focus ring on it. Copy it with `astryx template usePressFeedbackHookUsage <path>`; the docs-only playing card it shows becomes a `<Text>` placeholder for your own content.
