[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor`: asks that name parts by reference or in two states read as Core draws them. "The disabled one" after an element in a place ("the hovered item of menus … and the disabled one") is that element; "the others" are the part's other variants, or the part not in the state named; states said together read by their kind, a condition among them (checked, selected, disabled) meaning all at once and moment states alone (hovered, pressed) meaning each, with the other reading offered as a second line; a selected table row hovered sets the selected wash's token for hover; a switch's "on color" is its checked look; tighter table rows lower the cells' block padding one spacing step. A border asked of a list inside an overlay's surface (a typeahead's) points to the overlay's edge, and `set` refuses a border's color or width on a part that draws no border (a divider), offering the token its line reads.
