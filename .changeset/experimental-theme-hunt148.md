[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: a size ask with a bound that the part is already past the way the words ask ("rounder but at most 16px" on 42px corners) writes nothing and says so, never a move the other way; a direction beside a bound on a property with no scale of the theme's own (height) goes to the bound; "the body text … but at most 16px" is the base text size. A theme color with a way ("the accent color but lighter") is that color made that way in each mode. "The page color" as a gradient stop is the color the page is painted with, and the page is not changed. A role color made darker or lighter on a dark-only theme moves each of its tokens that way, its text included. "The same as" after a color property compares that color. "What color is the page" gives the page's color in each mode, and its pointer is the token that paints it.
