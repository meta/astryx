[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor set <theme> surfaces flat`: in a mode where the theme gives a panel a fill of its own (white cards in light mode), the panel shows that fill, so its pieces (a gray badge's pill, the muted fill) stay as they are there instead of being drawn in the card's color and vanishing on it.
