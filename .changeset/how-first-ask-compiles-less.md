[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` answers its first question in a process about 0.8 s sooner. An ask runs every pattern in the editor's part lexicon, and the first run of a pattern compiles it, so the first ask compiled about 1,700 patterns. A pattern made from a part, group or piece name now answers at once for a text without its word, without being compiled, as its match must hold that word. Answers do not change.
