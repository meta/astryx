[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: "clear the …" (or "clear my …", "clear all …") that leads an ask and names a background, border, shadow or another removable thing takes it away, as "remove" does ("clear the dark mode card background" gives the card a transparent fill in dark mode). Before, it read as a reset, answered that the theme already uses the base value, and left the base's own fill drawn. Clear stays a reset when that ask names the person's changes, edits, tweaks or other adjustments, overrides or settings, a color, or what someone added or put on ("clear my border changes", "clear the border I added"); "clear" said as a quality ("a clear border") reads as it did before.
