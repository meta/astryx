[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: "clear" said of a background, border, shadow or another removable thing takes it away, as "remove" does ("clear the dark mode card background" gives the card a transparent fill in dark mode). Before, it read as a reset, answered that the theme already uses the base value, and left the base's own fill drawn. "Clear my … changes" and "clear the overrides" stay a reset.
