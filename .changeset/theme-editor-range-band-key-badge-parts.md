[experimental] The theme editor knows Core's calendar range band and keyboard key badge parts. "The calendar range band" is `calendar-range`, and "range" is no longer corrected to "orange". "The key badges" are `kbd-key`; "keyboard keys", "the keyboard shortcut" and "the kbd" still read Kbd. A part that draws none of what is asked hands it to the one piece of its own inside it that draws it at rest, and names it: "make the keyboard keys gray" writes the fill on `kbd-key`. With several such parts, nothing is written and the answer names them. Spacing asked between the parts a holder lays out side by side ("more space between the keys in the keyboard shortcut", "between the tokens in the tokenizer") is that holder's gap. A fill set on `kbd` or on `calendar` was refused, because the key badges and the range band painted over it from a token; they are parts of their own now, so that fill is taken and paints the group's own box.
