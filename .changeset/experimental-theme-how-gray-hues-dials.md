[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: "neutral grays with blue type" and "neutral grays with a blue base" no longer read the blue as another part's color beside untinted grays. A "with" phrase after the grays counts as another part's color only when it names a part or a dial that takes a color (a palette dial other than neutral: accent, success, warning, danger, info); a dial that takes no color, said with a color, no longer counts.
