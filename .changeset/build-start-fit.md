[feat] `astryx build` says how sure it is of a closest start. When its page pick gives the start at least even odds against every other page template, the reason reads "The closest template, and a good fit for this idea." Below that it reads "The closest template, but a loose fit: compare it with the OTHER TEMPLATES below and start from the one whose layout fits best." A start from a template the word weights do not list (an integration's template, for example) keeps "The closest template; none is exactly this page." The start, its alternatives and every other field are unchanged; only the reason's words differ.
