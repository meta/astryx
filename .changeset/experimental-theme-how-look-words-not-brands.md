[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how`: a describing word said with "theme" ("minimal theme", "playful theme") is no longer answered as a brand whose colors are asked for. A picture named only by its file ("here is our brand board: ./look.png") now gets the answer for a picture, which says what to describe.

A built-in theme named as the look ("start from the butter look", "base butter") is now answered with the base (every setting is kept), instead of matching nothing. A base that is not installed is named with the line that installs it.
