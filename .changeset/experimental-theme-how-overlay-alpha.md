[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` reads a see-through color's alpha with the one shared color reader, so an `rgba()`, `hsla()` or `#rgba` overlay gets more transparent or more opaque as asked, and the check measures it the same way. After a removal, a noun with an adjective ("rounded corners") is an ask of its own: "no shadow and rounded corners on cards" removes the card's shadow and gives it round corners.
