[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor set` points at a line that works and says what happened. A word that names a dial and its value (`shadows none`, `elevation none`, `dark-mode true`) offers that dial's line (`set t surfaces flat`, `set t mode dark-only`) whatever the theme has now. A state a part does not have names the part drawn inside it that does ("today" on a calendar offers the line `--on calendar-day --state today`). A no-op `mode` or `base` set says nothing was built and no undo step was added, as every other no-op does. A theme with one color mode lists its own mode's token changes first.
