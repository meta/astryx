[fix] Coloured cards keep their pastel fill in dark mode and now draw dark primary text, secondary text and links on it. Inside them, a nested card, a field, inline code, an open menu, a selected segment, an input group addon, a step number, a banner and a striped table row draw the theme's own text again, and a transparent card keeps the card's ink. Secondary text on a selected row or a numbered citation, and code text on the page, step to the nearest stop that meets WCAG AA (4.5:1); inside a coloured card, up to two coloured cards deep, that secondary text takes a dark stop like the rest of the card's ink.
