[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` reads more of each ask the way it is said:

- **Unknown part names:** a word that names no part, in the part's place ("the zorp padding", "from the blorp"), is named in the answer, never changed for the whole theme.
- **Status excepts:** an except by status ("except error inputs") keeps that status's border on each input that draws one. An input that draws no border of its own is left without one.
- **Fainter text on a part:** a part's text made more transparent, less visible or fainter is its text color at a lower alpha, never the part's opacity. A toast's text uses the on-dark and on-light colors its surface gives it.
- **Danger badges:** "danger badge" is the badge's error variant.
- **Separators:** "separator" is the divider.
- **Keeping the focus ring:** "and keep the focus ring" is a keep.
- **Outline buttons on hover:** "outline buttons blue on hover" colors the main button's outline in that state.
- **Tokens:** "tokens square" changes the tokens.
- **Text on a part:** in "the text on primary buttons 4.5:1", the buttons hold the text; they are not the background it is measured against.
