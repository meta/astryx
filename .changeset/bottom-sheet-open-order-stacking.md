[feat] `BottomSheet` stacks by open order. A modal sheet opened while another modal sheet is open now stacks on top of it instead of presenting as a second, unrelated dialog: the covered sheet stays mounted with its state, recedes behind the top sheet (0.96 / −8px at depth 1, 0.92 / −16px from depth 2), and is blocked from pointer, keyboard, and assistive technology; only the bottom sheet's backdrop dims the page, so a stack no longer darkens per level; and Escape, a scrim tap, a swipe, and a platform close request pop only the top sheet, through that sheet's own `onOpenChange` and honouring its `purpose`. The top sheet is the most recently opened one still open, wherever the sheets are written — siblings, separate components, inside another sheet's content, or inside a `BottomSheetSwitcher` step; a whole switcher is one level, and switching its step keeps its place. When several sheets open in one update (a deep link), tree order decides and the first painted frame is already right: a sheet nested in another sheet's content no longer opens beneath its parent. Closing the top sheet returns focus into the sheet it reveals; closing every sheet of a stack in one update returns focus once, to the bottom sheet's `finalFocusRef` or opener, after the last dialog has closed (each sheet's own return used to be refused while the others were still modal). A sheet rendered inside a closing sheet's content now leaves with it instead of staying open invisibly, and comes back with the host if its own `isOpen` is still true (its state is never written for it); sheets above it that are not in its content stay open. Sheets with `hasScrim={false}` never stack and are never stacked on. No prop, type, or export changes; a modal sheet on its own behaves exactly as before (apart from the reduced-motion fix released alongside).
