[feat] Add Glass (`@astryxdesign/theme-glass`), a dark-only theme for dashboards and data-dense apps: a deep navy canvas with soft glows, glass cards with a light top edge, and one vivid hue per colored card. `<Card variant="blue">` tints the card and points its accent at the hue, so the charts, progress bars, and links inside it match. `astryx theme add glass` and `astryx discover` offer it.
