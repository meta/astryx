[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` reads a labelled look more closely:

- A label at the start of a clause or a line that is a setting's name, or a word only that setting has, followed by one of that setting's own values ("Density: roomy.", "Radius: small"), sets it to that value. A label that belongs to a part ("Body weight: bold.", "Buttons: soft.") keeps the part's reading.
- "sans" after a kind of typeface ("system sans", "humanist sans") is the typeface, never "without".
