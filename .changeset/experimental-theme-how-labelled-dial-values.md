[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` reads a labelled look more closely:

- A label at the start of a clause or a line that names the spacing or the corners setting, followed by one of that setting's own values ("Density: roomy.", "Radius: small"), sets it to that value when no part is named before it in the ask. A label that belongs to a part ("Body weight: bold.", "Buttons: soft."), or comes after a part is named ("Card" with "Radius: large" on the next line), keeps the part's reading.
- "sans" after humanist, geometric, grotesque or grotesk ("humanist sans") is the typeface, never "without".
