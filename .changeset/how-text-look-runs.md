[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` writes every look of a run said together right before a part's own text, led by a weight word ("tabs: bold 13px #3b5998 text", "tabs with bold uppercase 13px #3b5998 text"), instead of dropping its color. Links said with the top bar or top nav ("top bar #3b5998 with white bold 11px links") take their weight, size and case on its nav items instead of being refused, and the bar keeps their color as its link color where it holds them (with a sidebar named first, their color goes on the nav items instead of every link). Elsewhere, such as a run with no part of its own, a run placed in another part, or links said with another part, it reads as before.
