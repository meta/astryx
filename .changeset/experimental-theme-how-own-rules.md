[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor how` answers from what the theme shows on bases with rules of their own. An except keeps what the part shows: a whole-theme corner change pins the corner token on the part (a button group keeps its square joins), each heading level or variant with its own value keeps it, and a kept hover keeps its fill under Core's tint. A one-mode theme answers only for the mode it shows and never adds a mode. A gradient takes the colors named, in order. A text-size step starts from the base's body size, a variant's own literal fill is stepped from, and "more colorful" keeps each mode's accent hue. "Darker than the page" holds the page as a bound in each mode. "Remove the card border and shadow" removes both, "no bigger than 4px" is a target, a part's own tokens and its base rules count in the measured check, lighter or darker text with a mode named is read the same way by the answer and the check, a shadow typed for one mode goes into light-dark(), a length typed with no way is never a spacing step, and a color clause after an ask ("focus outline on text inputs, pink #db2777") is that ask's color.
