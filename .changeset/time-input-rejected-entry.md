[fix] TimeInput, DateTimeInput: a rejected typed time now meets WCAG 2.2 1.4.1 (Use of Color) and 3.3.1 (Error Identification). Before, every draft that was not an accepted time, an unfinished one such as `1` included, was marked invalid and announced on each keystroke, shown only as secondary-color text, and blur reverted it with no message; a native time edit outside `min` or `max` showed no message and its rejection cleared at blur. Now an unfinished time is never flagged: a time outside `min` or `max` shows once it is complete (its hours, both minute digits, both second digits when the field shows seconds, and in 12-hour text its day period unless the hour reads 0 or 13–23), and text that is not a time shows at Enter or blur. The rejected text stays in the field, and a standalone field shows the "Invalid time" message as its error status, with `aria-invalid` and one announcement; inside an InputGroup the rejection sets `aria-invalid` and is announced. Enter in TimeInput and in DateTimeInput's time field commits the typed time and still submits a surrounding form; in DateTimeInput's open preset-time list, Enter picks a time as before. A native time control restores the committed time at blur and keeps its rejection shown. A caller `error` status with a message still shows over the field's message, and the rejection is announced once; a `warning` or `success` status no longer hides a rejection. In DateTimeInput, a date rejection's message comes first and a time rejection under it is announced. What a form posts does not change: while blur keeps a rejected TimeInput draft, a hidden input carries the field's `name` with the committed time.
