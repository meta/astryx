[experimental] `astryx experimental theme editor …`: a saved twin of a setting in other letters (`accent` and `Accent`, `--color-accent` and `--Color-Accent`, `background-color` and `Background-Color` on a part) is that setting listed twice, which a set or undo of it fixes, instead of a refusal saying `Accent` is no dial. A field a saved setting writes several ways, none of them the editor's (`On` and `--on`, `thing` and `setting`), is read once from the first, with a note, and the next change writes it once. A saved field the editor does not read (`modes`) is named, with the field it is a typo of. An entry pasted in the saved form without a value (`undo t '{"thing":"padding","on":"card"}'`) is read by its thing.
